Privacy Policy for Vedic Bells

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Vedic Bells ("the app") calculates Vedic time windows such as Brahma Muhurta, Rahu Kalam, Abhijit Muhurta and Pradosham, and rings alarms at times you choose.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not collect, send or sell your personal data.
  • Everything the app needs is calculated and stored on your phone.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your settings: region, city, which alarms are on, offsets, weekdays, snooze length, alarm tone.
  • Your alarm schedule and, while an alarm is ringing, which alarm is ringing, so it can resume if the phone stops the app.
  • Your streaks: for each alarm, how many days in a row you pressed Stop, your best run, and the date of your last Stop.
  • These stay on your device. They are not included in Android backups or device-to-device transfers. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Location

  • If you tap "Use my location", the app asks for approximate location permission.
  • The location is read once and used only to calculate sunrise, sunset and the time windows.
  • It is rounded to about 1 km before it is saved, stored on your phone as part of your settings, and never sent to us or to any server run by us.
  • The location is read using your phone's built-in Android location service. The app does not use Google Play services or any other third-party library for this.
  • You can instead pick a city from the list and never grant the location permission.

Other permissions and why

  • Exact alarms: to ring at the exact minute each time window starts or ends.
  • Notifications: to show the ring screen and the Stop and Snooze buttons.
  • Full-screen alerts: to show the alarm over your lock screen when it rings.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to play the alarm sound reliably.
  • Vibration: to vibrate when the alarm rings.
  • Keep awake: to stop the phone sleeping while an alarm is ringing.
  • Run after restart: to restore your alarms after your phone restarts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
