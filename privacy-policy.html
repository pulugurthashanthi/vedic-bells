Privacy Policy for Vedic Bells

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Vedic Bells ("the app") shows the panchangam (tithi, nakshatram, festivals and more), calculates Vedic time windows such as Brahma Muhurta, Rahu Kalam, Abhijit Muhurta and Pradosham, rings alarms at times you choose, and reminds you of dates you save, such as birthdays and anniversaries.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts. You only sign in to Google if you choose Google Drive backup.
  • We, the developer, do not receive, read or sell your personal data. We run no servers.
  • The panchangam and alarms are calculated on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID, as explained below.
  • Your settings and saved dates can be backed up to your own Google account, as explained below.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your settings: language, region, city, which alarms are on, offsets, weekdays, snooze length, alarm tone, Mute all and Quiet hours.
  • Your Daily alarms: the time, weekdays and optional label of each alarm you add.
  • Your saved dates: the name you give each date (for example "Amma birthday"), the date and, if you add them, the time, the place (a city from the list) and any details you type (for example gotram or father's name), and how you want to be reminded.
  • Your alarm schedule and, while an alarm is ringing, which alarm is ringing, so it can resume if the phone stops the app.
  • The information shown on the home-screen widget (your next alarm and its time).
  • All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data, except copies in your own backups described below.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes the app's settings and saved dates (including your city and rounded location) in your phone's Google backup, so they come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot see it. The alarm schedule and ringing state are not backed up.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you tap "Back up now" under Saved dates and allow access, the app saves your settings and saved dates as one file in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can read that folder, and the data is sent over an encrypted connection. After the first backup, later changes are backed up automatically. We never receive this data. You can delete it any time: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Vedic Bells → Delete hidden app data.

Location

  • If you tap "Use my location", the app asks for approximate location permission.
  • The location is read once, rounded to about 1 km, and used only to calculate sunrise, sunset, the panchangam and the time windows.
  • It is saved on your phone as part of your settings and is included in the backups described above. It is never sent to us.
  • You can instead type and pick a city and never grant the location permission.

Ads

  • The app shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob. For the first 21 days after you install the app, only banner ads are shown.
  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information in ads.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Ads are never shown on the alarm ring screen or over the lock screen.

Notifications

  • The app can show a morning panchangam notification, a note on the evening before a big festival, and reminders for your saved dates. Each can be switched off in the app.

Other permissions and why

  • Exact alarms: to ring at the exact minute each time window starts or ends.
  • Notifications: to show the ring screen, the Stop and Snooze buttons, and the reminders above.
  • Full-screen alerts: to show the alarm over your lock screen when it rings.
  • Foreground service: to keep an alarm ringing after it starts, so the sound does not stop when the screen is off or the app is closed.
  • Vibration: to vibrate when the alarm rings.
  • Keep awake: to stop the phone sleeping while an alarm is ringing.
  • Run after restart: to restore your alarms after your phone restarts.
  • Internet: to load ads and, only if you choose it, to back up to Google Drive.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described under Ads.

Children

The app is made for families. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children, and we do not receive any data from the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
